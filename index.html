How to read these numbers

    Sources: this plan's sales brochure, linked under "Plan at a glance"; fund returns from SBI Life fund factsheets and Value Research data, both as carried on Policybazaar's SBI Life fund pages. For a customer, use the policy-specific benefit illustration generated by SBI Life.

    In unit-linked policies the investment risk in the investment portfolio is borne by the policyholder. Unit-linked products do not offer any liquidity during the first five years of the contract.

    Plan details were read from SBI Life's sales brochures and product pages on 8 and 9 October 2026. Each plan's "at a glance" names its source. Products, rates and terms change, so check the current brochure before quoting, and give the customer SBI Life's policy-specific benefit illustration before the sale.
